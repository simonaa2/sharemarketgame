📚 Stage 5 Commerce / Stage 6 Economics Teaching Sequence
📅 3-Week Student Self-Directed Program: Investing & Financial Independence

Students complete this 9-lesson program inside their "🎯 3-Week Missions & Independence" tab. Their practical trades and written reflections sync live to your Student Inspector below.

WEEK 1 (Lessons 1–3) Foundations & ASX

Wealth Foundations, Risk & Core ASX Portfolio

  • Lesson 1 (Why Invest & Independence): Inflation (~3% p.a.) vs compound growth & the "4% Financial Independence Rule" (25× annual expenses). Students model goals in the FI Calculator.
  • Lesson 2 (ASX Blue Chips & Sectors): Defensive (CBA, WOW, CSL, TLS) vs Cyclical (BHP, FMG, QAN, FLT) shares & franked dividends. Task: Buy 2+ ASX stocks.
  • Lesson 3 (25% Cap & Brokerage): Unsystematic risk, $10 brokerage drag on small trades, and position sizing. Task: Hold 3+ diversified stocks.
👨‍🏫 Teacher Action: Have students log in with class code 10COMM1 and complete Week 1 in the 🎯 3-Week Missions tab.
WEEK 2 (Lessons 4–6) Global & Macro

Wall Street (NYSE/NASDAQ), FX Risk & Macro Shocks

  • Lesson 4 (Global Markets & AUD/USD): Why invest beyond Australia (<2% of global market) into US tech/global leaders, and how AUD/USD currency movements impact returns. Task: Execute 1+ US trade.
  • Lesson 5 (Valuation Metrics): P/E ratios, Market Cap, and 52-week ranges (Growth vs Value). Task: Complete 3+ Stock Journal entries with target prices.
  • Lesson 6 (Macro Catalysts): RBA interest rate hikes, inflation, and sector rotation. Task: Written catalyst impact reflection.
👨‍🏫 Teacher Action: Mid-Week 2, use the ⚡ Trigger Macro Catalyst bar above to broadcast "🏦 RBA Hikes Cash Rate (+25 bps)" or "📉 Tech Valuation Pullback".
WEEK 3 (Lessons 7–9) Alpha & Independence

Passive ETFs vs Active Alpha, Rebalancing & Final Audit

  • Lesson 7 (Active vs Passive Index): Evaluating portfolio "Alpha" against the S&P/ASX 200 (8.2% p.a.) and why low-cost Index ETFs suit long-term independence.
  • Lesson 8 (Rebalancing & Psychology): Avoiding FOMO & panic selling, reinvesting dividends, and trimming/rebalancing. Task: 4+ total trades executed.
  • Lesson 9 (Personal Independence Blueprint): Dollar-Cost Averaging, Superannuation, and post-school wealth plan + CSV Ledger export.
👨‍🏫 Teacher Action: Broadcast "💰 ASX Semi-Annual Dividend (2%)", then click 🔍 Inspect on each student below to grade out of 20 and export CSV.
