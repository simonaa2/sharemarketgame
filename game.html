📚 Commerce & Economics Investor Toolkit

Key financial concepts to help you build a resilient, high-performing share portfolio.

🧺

Portfolio Diversification (25% Rule)

"Don't put all your eggs in one basket." Under our classroom rules, no single stock can exceed 25% of your total portfolio. Spreading investments across different sectors cushions against company-specific shocks.

💱

Currency Risk (AUD vs. USD)

When investing in US shares (NYSE/NASDAQ), your returns depend on both the stock price and the AUD/USD exchange rate. If the Aussie Dollar falls against the US Dollar, your US holdings gain value when converted back to AUD.

💸

Brokerage Impact & Over-Trading

Every trade incurs a $10 AUD transaction fee. Day-trading tiny share parcels frequently can quickly erode student profits. Consider position sizing before placing orders.

🐂

Market Cycles & Benchmarks

Compare your portfolio's performance against the S&P/ASX 200 benchmark line. Generating "Alpha" means delivering a return higher than simply holding the broader market index fund.
