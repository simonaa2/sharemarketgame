Part 3: Critical Analysis — Interpreting Key Financial Statistics

1. Deconstructing Beta (β) & Systematic Volatility Risk

Compare the Beta (β) of your two companies. What does a Beta of 1.0 signify? If a sudden market shock causes the ASX 200 / S&P 500 to plummet by 10%, which stock would you expect to drop further, and why?

2. P/E Ratio: Value vs High Growth Expectations

Why are investors often willing to pay a high P/E ratio (e.g. 35+) for a US tech company like Nvidia, while paying a much lower P/E (e.g. 12-16) for a bank or miner? What happens to the high P/E stock if company earnings fall short of expectations?

3. The "Share Price Fallacy" (Market Cap vs Share Price)

Company X trades at $120.00 with 10 million shares. Company Y trades at $15.00 with 500 million shares. Calculate each company's Market Cap. Explain why looking at share price alone does not indicate how big or valuable a company is.
