# 3-Week Unit Plan: Share Market Investing & Pathway to Financial Independence

**Target Cohort**: Year 9/10 Commerce & Year 11 Preliminary Economics  
**Duration**: 3 Weeks (9 × 50–60 minute lessons, self-directed & teacher-facilitated)  
**Platform**: Share Market Arena (`index.html` for Students, `teacher.html` for Educator Command Center)  
**Teacher Passwords**: `market10` or `hscsando1603`

---

## Unit Overview & Educational Philosophy
This 3-week unit connects active share market simulation ($150,000 AUD virtual capital across the **ASX**, **NYSE**, and **NASDAQ**) with real-world **personal financial independence**. Students learn not only *how* to research and trade equities, but *why* long-term compounding, diversification, fee control, and passive index investing are essential life skills for financial autonomy.

All 9 lessons are built directly into the student app under the **`🎯 3-Week Missions & Independence`** tab. Student progress (`X/9` modules) and written reflections automatically sync to the **Teacher Command Center (`teacher.html`)**.

---

## Week 1: Foundations of Wealth, Risk & Building Your Core ASX Portfolio
*Focus: Why cash loses to inflation, how compound returns create financial independence, and how to construct a diversified Australian blue-chip portfolio.*

### Lesson 1: Why Invest? Inflation, Compounding & Financial Independence (`w1_m1`)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - **Purchasing Power & Inflation**: Why $150,000 left in a 0% cash account loses ~3% of its real value each year.
  - **Compound Growth**: Earning returns on your past returns over 10–30 years.
  - **Financial Independence & The "4% Rule"**: Accumulating an investment portfolio equal to **25× your annual living expenses** so passive returns and dividends fund your lifestyle independently.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - Open the **`🎯 3-Week Missions & Independence`** tab and use the **Interactive Financial Independence & Compound Growth Simulator**.
  - Complete **Lesson 1 Reflection**: Explain why holding 100% cash is a long-term risk and define what achieving Financial Independence in your 20s or 30s would allow you to do.

### Lesson 2: Australian Blue Chips, Sectors & Dividend Income (`w1_m2`)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - How the **Australian Securities Exchange (ASX)** works (`10:00 AM – 4:00 PM AEST`).
  - **Defensive Sectors** (Consumer Staples `WOW.AX`, `COL.AX`; Healthcare `CSL.AX`, `COH.AX`; Telcos `TLS.AX`) vs **Cyclical Sectors** (Materials/Mining `BHP.AX`, `RIO.AX`, `FMG.AX`; Aviation/Travel `QAN.AX`, `FLT.AX`).
  - **Dividends & Franking Credits**: Receiving a share of company profits in cash.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - Research 4 ASX companies in the **`🔍 Market Explorer`** and add them to their Watchlist.
  - **Practical Mission (Auto-Verified)**: Execute at least **2 ASX Buy Orders** across different sectors, writing a 15+ character investment thesis for each.
  - Complete **Lesson 2 Reflection** identifying whether their chosen ASX companies are defensive or cyclical.

### Lesson 3: Diversification (The 25% Rule) & Brokerage Drag (`w1_m3`)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - **Unsystematic (Company) Risk vs Systematic (Market) Risk**.
  - **The 25% Position Cap**: Why no single company can exceed $37,500 AUD (25% of $150,000) in the simulator.
  - **Brokerage Drag**: How a $10 AUD flat fee equals a 5% loss on a $200 trade, vs <0.1% on an $11,000 position.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - **Practical Mission (Auto-Verified)**: Build a diversified portfolio of **3+ active share holdings** across distinct industries.
  - Complete **Lesson 3 Reflection** on position sizing and fee management.

## Week 2: Global Markets, Currency Risk & Macroeconomic Shocks
*Focus: Expanding internationally to Wall Street (NYSE/NASDAQ), understanding AUD/USD exchange rate risk, reading valuation multiples (P/E), and reacting to macroeconomic events.*

### Lesson 4: Going Global — Wall Street (NYSE & NASDAQ) & Currency Risk (`w2_m1`)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Australia makes up **<2% of the global share market** and is heavily concentrated in banks and mining.
  - **NYSE & NASDAQ** offer exposure to global technology, AI semiconductors, and consumer leaders (`AAPL`, `MSFT`, `NVDA`, `GOOGL`, `AMZN`, `COST`, `V`).
  - **Foreign Exchange (`AUD/USD`) Risk**: How currency movements affect AUD portfolio returns.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - **Practical Mission (Auto-Verified)**: Execute at least **1 US Share Trade (NYSE or NASDAQ)**.
  - Complete **Lesson 4 Reflection** explaining how their US holding diversifies their Australian portfolio and how an AUD/USD rise from `0.65` to `0.75` impacts AUD value.

### Lesson 5: Reading Financial Metrics — P/E Ratio, Market Cap & 52-Week Range (`w2_m2`)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - **Market Capitalisation**: Total company value (`Share Price × Total Shares`).
  - **Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio**: Growth stocks (high P/E, e.g., 30x–60x) vs Value/Income stocks (lower P/E, e.g., 10x–18x).
  - **52-Week High/Low**: Assessing current price momentum and support/resistance.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - **Practical Mission (Auto-Verified)**: Complete full entries in the **`📔 Investment Journal`** tab (Investment Thesis, Key Risk Factors, and Target Exit Price) for at least **3 stocks**.
  - Complete **Lesson 5 Reflection** comparing a high-P/E growth stock and a lower-P/E value stock in their portfolio.

### Lesson 6: Interest Rates (RBA), Inflation & Economic Catalysts (`w2_m3`)
- **Teacher Classroom Action**:
  - Log into `teacher.html` and use **⚡ Trigger Macro Catalyst** to broadcast a live economic shock to all student screens (e.g., **`🏦 RBA Hikes Cash Rate (+25 bps)`** or **`📉 Tech & AI Valuation Pullback`**).
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - Observe the live breaking news banner on their trading screen.
  - Complete **Lesson 6 Reflection**: Analyze how rising interest rates or sector pullbacks impact their specific holdings and justify whether they chose to hold, buy the dip, or rebalance.

## Week 3: Passive vs Active Investing, Rebalancing & Your Independence Roadmap
*Focus: Comparing active stock picking against passive index funds (S&P/ASX 200 Alpha), controlling investor psychology, and designing a real-world post-school wealth plan.*

### Lesson 7: Generating "Alpha" vs Passive Index Investing (`w3_m1`)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - **What is Alpha?**: Your portfolio's excess return above (or below) the passive **S&P/ASX 200 Benchmark (8.2% p.a.)**.
  - **Active vs Passive Investing**: Why low-cost Index ETFs (e.g. VAS/A200 for ASX 200, IVV/VGS for global shares) beat 80%+ of active traders over 10–20 years.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - Inspect their **`Alpha vs ASX 200`** KPI card and portfolio chart benchmark line.
  - Complete **Lesson 7 Reflection** evaluating whether their active stock picks beat or trailed the passive index benchmark and why.

### Lesson 8: Strategic Rebalancing, Dividends & Investor Psychology (`w3_m2`)
- **Teacher Classroom Action**:
  - Broadcast **`💰 ASX Semi-Annual Dividend (2%)`** from `teacher.html` so students receive a 2% cash dividend payout across their active holdings.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Avoiding **FOMO** (chasing hype) and **Panic Selling**.
  - Reinvesting dividends (compounding) and trimming losers or overweight winners.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - **Practical Mission (Auto-Verified)**: Have at least **4 total trades executed** (including dividend reinvestment or strategic rebalancing).
  - Complete **Lesson 8 Reflection** reviewing their best and worst trades.

### Lesson 9: My Real-World Financial Independence Blueprint & Final Audit (`w3_m3`)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - **Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)**: Automating a set % of every paycheque into investments regardless of market noise.
  - **Superannuation**: Understanding how youth + high-growth/low-fee super options compound from your first part-time job.
- **Student Activity in Game**:
  - Complete **Lesson 9 Reflection**: Write a personal **3-Step Financial Independence Plan** for ages 16–30 (savings rate, ETF/share mix, and behavioural rules).
  - Open **`📜 Trade Ledger`** and click **`📥 Download Trade History (CSV)`** for final portfolio submission.
- **Teacher Final Action**:
  - Click **`🔒 Freeze Trading`** on `teacher.html`, click **`🔍 Inspect & Grade`** on each student to review their 9 workbook responses, stock journals, and trade ledger, assign their mark out of 20, and click **`📥 Export Class CSV`**.
